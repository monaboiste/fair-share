# Fair Share

Fair Share helps groups track shared expenses and work out who owes whom.

## Language

**Participant**:
A person included in a group's expense accounting, regardless of who uses the app to record Expenses.

**Inactive Participant**: A Participant who remains part of the group's expense accounting but is not eligible to pay or
receive a Share on new Expenses.

**Expense**: A described outlay incurred on a specified date, paid by exactly one Participant, and allocated among one
or more Participants. The payer does not have to receive a Share.

**Split method**:
The rule for dividing an Expense among the Participants who receive Shares.

**Share**:
The part of an Expense assigned to one Participant.

**Settlement currency**:
The currency in which a group's balances are expressed.

**Rate snapshot**:
The currency pair and exchange rate used to value a foreign-currency Expense in the group's Settlement currency.

**Settlement proposal**:
A suggested set of payments between Participants to clear their outstanding balances. It does not record payments made.
