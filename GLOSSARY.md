# Fair Share

Fair Share helps groups track shared expenses and work out who owes whom.

## Language

**Group**: A named unit of shared expense accounting with its own Participants, Expenses, and Settlement currency.

**Participant**: A person included in a Group's expense accounting, regardless of who uses the app to record Expenses.

**Inactive Participant**: A Participant who remains part of the Group's expense accounting but is not eligible to pay or
receive a Share on new Expenses.

**Expense**: A described outlay incurred on a specified date, paid by exactly one Participant, and allocated among one
or more Participants. The payer does not have to receive a Share.

**Expense reversal**: An accounting action that cancels a recorded Expense's effect by negating its original postings
without erasing the Expense. _Avoid_: Deletion, refund

**Replacement Expense**: A new Expense that corrects a reversed Expense and links back to it.

**Split method**: The rule for dividing an Expense among the Participants who receive Shares.

**Share**: The part of an Expense assigned to one Participant.

**Settlement currency**: The currency in which a Group's balances are expressed.

**Participant balance**: A Participant's net credits minus debits in the Group's Settlement currency. A positive balance
means the Participant is owed money; a negative balance means they owe money.

**Rate snapshot**: The currency pair and exchange rate used to value a foreign-currency Expense in the Group's
Settlement currency.

**Settlement proposal**: A suggested set of payments between Participants to clear their outstanding balances. It does
not record payments made.
