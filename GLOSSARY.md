# Fair Share

Fair Share helps groups track shared expenses and work out who owes whom.

## Language

**Participant**:
A person included in a group's expense accounting. A Participant can pay for an Expense, receive a Share, or both.

**Expense**: A described outlay incurred on a specified date, paid by exactly one Participant, and allocated among one
or more Participants. The payer does not have to receive a Share.

**Share**:
The part of an Expense assigned to one Participant.

**Settlement currency**:
The currency in which balances are expressed.

**Settlement proposal**:
A suggested set of payments between Participants to clear their outstanding balances. It does not record payments made.
